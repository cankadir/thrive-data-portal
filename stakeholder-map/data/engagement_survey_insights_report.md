## 3️⃣ Thrive Tool Engagement

### Adoption Rate
| Used Thrive Tools? | Count | % |
|---|---|---|
| **No** | 51 | **66%** |
| **Yes** | 26 | **34%** |

### Most Used Thrive Tools
1. **Interactive Map: Thrive Regional** — most popular
2. **Dashboard: Cradle of Southern Appalachia** — second most
3. **Dashboard: County-by-County Data** — third
4. **Dashboard: Transportation & Infrastructure** — moderate
5. **Other tools** — minimal usage

### Role of Thrive in Projects
- Only **20 out of 77 (26%)** said Thrive played a role in their projects
- Where Thrive played a role, it was primarily **advisory and data support**

### Key Challenges (verbatim themes)
1. **Navigation & UX** — "Labels are wonky", "hard to navigate"
2. **Geographic coverage gaps** — "only covers one-third of our counties"
3. **Data format limitations** — "couldn't find download option", "need to filter"
4. **Training needed** — "training needed sometimes", "tuned for experts"
5. **Specific data gaps** — supply chain/freight, transportation origin-destination
6. **No issues reported** — some users had positive experiences

## 5️⃣ Key Recommendations

### Priority 1: Onboarding & Adoption
**66% haven't used Thrive tools.** Invest in:
- Onboarding workshops/webinars
- Video tutorials for each tool
- One-on-one demos for the 47 willing to follow up

### Priority 2: Improve UX & Accessibility
- Simplify labels for non-expert/rural audiences
- Add clear download buttons and data export options
- Improve navigation and search within tools

### Priority 3: Expand Geographic Coverage
- Address the gap for counties not currently covered
- Partner with NWGRC and other regional organizations
- Include origin-destination and freight data

### Priority 4: Focus on Interactive Maps & Dashboards
These are the two most requested formats — prioritize development here

### Priority 5: Leverage Beta Testers
**60 people (78%) willing to beta test.** Build a testing cohort:
- Recruit diverse sectors and geographies
- Use for iterative Hub development
- Create feedback loops

### Priority 6: Address Data Gaps
- Supply chain and freight volume data (domestic & international)
- Transportation origin-destination data
- Conservation progress metrics
- Consistent data standards across state lines (TN, GA, AL)
